Joacă: the pop-up

No new screen. Tapping the key opens one card above the bar, chosen by what the player has going on. Try it in the phone.

How it behaves

  • The key turns like in a lock, then becomes an X. Tap the X, the dimmed area, or drag the card down to close.
  • Registered: a ticket with a live countdown, your team and the meeting point.
  • Start window: the same ticket, now with the minutes left to enter and the start-code button.
  • Playing: mission progress, time left, your place, and Continue. The key has a red ring while you play.
  • Not registered: a suggested event. "Alt joc" rolls the dice and flips to a random one.
  • From the key: the card grows out of the button with a small bounce.
  • Map unfolds: the card opens like a folded map, the folds fade.
  • Draw a card: three cards fan out, shuffle, and the middle one flips over.
  • The phone gives a short buzz when the card opens.
