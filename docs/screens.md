# Screen-by-screen behaviour

Companion to `features.md` (route table + architecture). Here: what each screen shows/does, its user-visible Romanian strings, validation and notable behaviour. Paths are under `lib/features/`.

## Entry / auth
- **LandingPage** (`loader_page/landing_page.dart`) — full-screen Lottie; post-frame calls `AuthUtil.checkUserAndRedirect`. Signed out → `splash`; admin → `adminHome`; user → `home`; if the Firestore `users` doc is missing it does nothing (stuck on the loader).
- **SplashScreen** (`splash/splash.dart`) — SVG background, logo, "Bine ai venit!", buttons "Loghează-te" (→ login) and "Inregistrează-te" (→ register).
- **LogInScreen** (`log-in/`) — email + password with validators ("Te rugăm să introduci adresa de email/parola"); "Logare cu Google"; **"Logare cu Facebook" is a placeholder (`onTap: () {}`)**; "Ai uitat parola?" → forgot-password. Success → `pushReplacementNamed(Routes.redirect)` (re-runs role redirect). Errors shown as raw `e.toString()` in a SnackBar (English messages from `AuthenticationRepository`).
- **Register** (`sign-up/screen/register.dart`) — chooser: email (→ `signup`), Google, Facebook (button present, same placeholder pattern), link to login.
- **RegisterScreen** (`sign-up/screen/sign_up.dart`) — email (regex), first name, last name, password (≥ 6 chars), confirm password. `SignUpController.register` → `SignUpService.register` creates auth user + `users` doc (role defaults USER, stores FCM token). SnackBar 'Account created successfully!' → `redirect`. Note: `userRepo.saveUser` is **not awaited**.
- **ForgotPasswordScreen** — email → `FirebaseAuth.sendPasswordResetEmail`; maps `invalid-email` / `user-not-found` to RO messages. `ForgotPasswordService.sendLink` is the thin wrapper over `sendPasswordResetEmail`.

## Player
- **Home** (`home/screen/home.dart`, the Acasă tab) — mockup 02. `HomeHeader` (panel, 1 px bottom border, under the status bar: teal route mark 20, "TeamXperience" 15 w500, a 32 px `iconButtonBg` bell; a 7 px `live` dot while a news item is newer than the last bell tap, kept on the device by `NewsSeenStore`; the bell opens Știri). Then, before a game: `GreetingStats` ("Salut, {firstName}", three `MiniStatCard`s 1 : 1 : 1.3 — games "jocuri", km "pe jos", the level from `users/{uid}.xp` with its progress bar; zero until step 7 writes them); the hero card (margin 12/14/0); "Evenimente noi" (`SectionHeader` top 16, "Vezi toate" → Evenimente tab) with the open events not registered for (`sectionEvents(...).open`) as `EventMiniCard`s in a sideways row (date "21 nov", "3,2 km · 1 h 30"; tap → `eventDetails`). Hero cards (`HomeHeroSection` for `resolveHomeHero`): `NextGameCard` (navy radius 22: badge "Următorul tău joc", name, "Sâm 14 nov · 10:00 · {punctDeStart}", `CountdownBoxes` days/hours/min, and once the team is loaded a band "Pregătirea echipei {team}" with a `CheckpointLine` Înscriși → Echipă completă (members ≥ size) → Cod de start (from the event's day); tap → `registerTeamDetails`), `StartingSoonCard` ("A început", "Mai ai N min să intri", "Introdu codul de start"), `RecommendedCard` ("Recomandat pentru tine", day line + route facts, "Înscrie-ți echipa" → event page). During a game (`LiveHero`) the greeting and new events are hidden: `LiveGameCard` (`liveCard` fill: "LIVE", the time left `H:MM:SS` in Monospace + "rămase" (start + 3 h − now, never negative), "Echipa {team} · misiunea N din M", `MissionProgressLine`, "Continuă jocul" → `eventGame`, chat button → team chat), "Clasament live" (`LiveLeaderboardSection`: the top three, or the top two and the team; "{team} · voi" highlighted; score = the mission a team is on "4/6", "–" without missions; hidden while the team is not in the stream; "Tot" → `showLeaderboardSheet` with every team) and "Echipa ta" (`TeamNowCard`: avatars of the members seen in the last 2 min, "3 jucători activi", "Ultima misiune rezolvată acum 6 min"). Teams come from `HomeLiveController` (streams the live event's teams, ranked by `rankTeams`); the live sections refresh every 30 s. Pull-to-refresh (`HomeController.refreshData`); a spinner under the header only while there is no cached data.
- **EventDetailsScreen** — the page of mockup 01 (`docs/roadmap/mockups/01-event-details.html`), built by `EventPageView` from plain values; `EventDetailsController` loads the team count and the player's own team and does the navigation. Top to bottom: hero (picture or navy rings, back + share, badge from `heroBadge`: "Înscrieri deschise · încă N zile" / "ultima zi" / "Înscrieri de pe 1 nov" / LIVE / "Înscrieri închise"), route strip ("4,5 km · 6 misiuni", start and finish, "Hartă" opens the start pin in Google Maps), facts (date tile, duration, difficulty dots, teams), then a route trail of sections: Povestea (folds to 3 lines, "Citește tot"), Ce vă așteaptă (public mission teasers, "Încă N misiuni secrete", puzzle-kind chips), Cum jucați, Logistică (meeting point, "fii acolo la", enrolment deadline, what to bring), Organizator (initials, events count, message button opening the team chat when the player has a team). The bottom bar comes from `signupBar`: register while enrolment is open (respecting `maxTeams`), "Echipa ta" when registered, "Introdu codul" in the first hour, "Continuă" while playing, and plain status rows when not open yet, closed, full or over. Admin variant: `MyAppBar` with the dropdown menu, no bar, and the "Vezi echipele înscrise" / "Gestionează evenimentul" buttons under the trail.
- **RegisterTeamScreen** — one short screen, the left phone of mockup 04 (`docs/roadmap/mockups/04-register-and-join-team.html`), with a `ScreenBar` (× , "Înscrie echipa", "{event} · {day month}"). `RegisterForm` (pure widget fed by `RegisterTeamController`): "Numele echipei" (teal paw badge + input; teal border and a check once the name is free; `FieldStatusLine` under it: "Verificăm numele…" / "Numele e liber" / "Numele e luat" / "Numele are 2–30 de litere, fără /" / "Nu am putut verifica numele"), "Câți veți fi?" (`SizePicker` from `teamSizeMin` to `teamSizeMax`, starting at the maximum capped at 4), "Telefonul căpitanului" (phone keyboard, hint "0722 123 456"; name and email come from the account), two consent rows "Accept regulamentul" (required) and "Sunt de acord cu foto și video" (optional), and the navy button "Înscrie echipa", greyed out until the name is checked and free, the phone is filled and the rules are ticked (`RegisterTeamController.blocker` names the first missing thing). The name is checked on the server (`TeamService.isTeamNameFree`) 400 ms after typing stops (`TeamNameCheck`; only the last answer counts); bad names are refused locally with `cleanTeamName` / `teamNameProblem` (`lib/utils/team_names.dart`, the twin of `functions/src/teams/names.ts`). `submit` calls `TeamService.registerTeam`, then `UserProvider.addRegisteredEvent`; errors show `MySnackBars.errorSnackBar` with `teamErrorText`, and a `nameTaken` error also marks the field. On success the **same screen** shows the navy `InviteCodeCard` under the form (caption "Gata. Trimite codul coechipierilor", the code "VLP-482", "WhatsApp" → `ShareService.shareInvite`, "Copiază" → clipboard + snackbar "Cod copiat", footer `AvatarStack` + "1 din N în echipă") and scrolls to it; the form becomes read-only and the button disappears. The × (and the back button, once registered) goes back before registering; after registering it pops to the first route and opens `eventDetails` afresh with `{'event': event, 'isAdmin': false}`. There is no separate invite-result screen.
- **JoinTeamScreen** (`Routes.joinTeam`, no arguments) — the right phone of mockup 04. `ScreenBar` (arrow-left, "Intră într-o echipă"). Top: a 56 px `iconButtonBg` circle with the ticket icon, "Ai primit un cod?" (15 px, w500), "Îl găsești în mesajul de la căpitan.", then `CodeBoxesInput(length: 6, dashAfter: 3)`. A complete, well-formed code (3 letters + 3 digits) is looked up with `TeamService.previewInvite` (a 6-character code of the wrong shape is refused locally as "Codul nu există"); the error text (`teamErrorText`, e.g. "Codul nu există", "Nu a mers. Verifică internetul.") is written under the boxes in `danger` and every box border turns `danger` until the code is edited. When found, `InvitePreviewCard` shows an accent strip with the event name, the team badge and name, "Căpitan: {name} · Sâm 14 nov, 10:00" (`shortDateTime`), an `AvatarStack` with the open places (the player's own highlighted) and `slotLine` ("2 din 4 · tu ești al 3-lea"); without an event only the captain and the members show. Bottom (pinned): `NavyButton` "Intră în {crewName}" (greyed "Intră în echipă" until found; spinner while joining) and "Evenimentul apare la Înscrierile tale și primești notificări.". A full team ("Echipa e completă") is refused locally without calling `joinTeam`; a second tap while joining does nothing. On success each event id is added to `UserProvider`, and the event page of the first one found in `EventList` replaces the screen (`{'event': event, 'isAdmin': false}`); with none the screen goes back to the Echipe screen it was opened from (`popUntil` `Routes.teams`; a new one is pushed only if none is below). Leaving while the join is on its way still adds the events to the player; editing the code while joining is ignored; a code of a team the player is already in shows "Ești deja în echipă" under the boxes instead of the preview.
- **TeamsScreen** "Echipe" (`Routes.teams`, no arguments; the Echipe tab of `UserShell`, and opened on its own after joining with no known event) — the right phone of mockup 03. A header (panel, 1 px bottom border, 17 px "Echipe", `AppChip` plain "Echipă nouă" with a plus) and, for each crew the player belongs to (`crews` where `memberUids` contains the uid, by name), a navy card (radius 22, margin 12 top / 14 sides): a 44 px `tickOnNavy` badge with the paw, the name (15 px w500) and "Căpitan: {captain} · {n} membri" ("1 membru" for one; just the count when the captain is not among the members), an `AvatarStack` (28 px, on navy, up to 4 members, no empty places) and a dashed code box (8% `onNavy` fill, 35% dashed border) with "Cod invitație", the code in mono (e.g. "VLP-482") and the share and copy icons (copy → clipboard + snackbar 'Cod copiat'). At the foot the `JoinLink` card (ticket icon, "Ai primit un cod de la altă echipă?", "Intră"; the whole card opens `joinTeam`). With no crews: "Nu ești încă într-o echipă." and the same link, and "Echipă nouă" stays in the header. "Echipă nouă" opens `NewCrewSheet`: title "Echipă nouă", name field ("Numele echipei", 2–30 characters), `NavyButton` "Creează" (greyed until the name is valid, spinner while creating, `teamErrorText` under the field on a failure); on success the sheet shows the new crew's `InviteCodeCard`. The screen has no back arrow (as in the mockup); the system back returns. Stats tiles and the season leaderboard are not drawn yet (step 7).
- **TeamDetailsScreen** ("Confirmare înscriere") — shows event card + team fields; "Componență echipă" is an `AvatarStack` of the members with their names under it. "Editează/Anulează" toggles edit mode, in which only "Telefon" becomes a text field (name, members, captain name and email are read-only); "Salvează schimbările" → `TeamControllerDetails.savePhone` (error SnackBar 'Eroare la salvare'). For the captain of a team that has an invite code, a navy `InviteCodeCard` ("Codul echipei", "WhatsApp" → `ShareService.shareInvite`, "Copiază" → clipboard + 'Cod copiat') sits above the buttons. "Distribuie detaliile" opens the share bottom sheet. Uses `FutureBuilder(future: controller.initializeTeam())` **created inside `build`, so every rebuild (e.g. toggling edit) re-fetches the team**.
- **EnterCode** — one text field, hint "Introdu codul aici", button → `EnterCodeController.navigateToGame`: if `text == event.startCode` (case-sensitive, untrimmed) loads the team, `startTeam`, pushes `eventGame`. A wrong code gives **no feedback**. `team!` will throw if the user has no team.
- **EventGameScreen** — event landing image with back arrow, vertical list of `MissionStateWidget` (one per `nrMissions`, state from `Team.missionProgress`), connectors between them, "Ai nevoie de ajutor ?" chat shortcut (→ `chatScreen`). Tapping a mission pushes `puzzleGame` and updates local `team`/mission states from the returned `Team`. On `eventEnded` shows dialog "Ne pare rău / S-a terminat evenimentul." and pops to the first route. Admin preview (menu "Vezi Eveniment") enters with `team == null` → every mission is locked.
- **PuzzleGameScreen** — no progress bar; the body changes per puzzle type (see `features.md`). Components fade in with staggered `Opacity`. When a completed mission is revisited, all buttons just advance. Returns the updated `Team` to the mission list (`null` on write failure).
- **GeolocationPuzzleWidget** — flutter_map showing the target circle (`_circleRadiusMeters`) and the player; live distance via `Geolocator.getPositionStream(distanceFilter: 2)`; walking route from `RouteService` (throttled); enables the "solved" action when inside the circle (or mission already completed).
- **ImageUploadPuzzleWidget** — see "Photo-puzzle approval flow" in `features.md`. Banner strings: 'Se așteaptă aprobarea organizatorului...', 'Imaginea a fost aprobată!', 'Imaginea a fost respinsă. Încarcă alta.'; button 'Încarcă altă poză'. Requires `currentUser!`.
- **ChatScreen** (shared admin/player) — `MessagesStream` (nested StreamBuilders: team doc for pending approval + messages ordered by time) with `MessageBubble` / `ImageMessageBubble` (tap → `FullScreenImage`; Aprobă/Respinge buttons only for admins on the latest pending image), text field + send icon. Sending trims nothing and does not block empty text.
- **DateRangeSelectionScreen** (`calendar`) — custom calendar grid, tap start then end date (earlier second tap becomes new start), header 'Selectează perioada' / 'Selectează data de sfârșit', clear, "Salvează perioada" pops `{startDate,endDate}`.
- **FilterEventsScreen** — event type dropdown (admin only; "Public"/"Privat"), period field → calendar, "Salvează modificările" pops the filter map. **Evenimente tab** (`events_tab`, also at `eventDashboardUser`) — mockup 03: search button, Listă/Hartă switch, filter chips that combine (difficulties OR, kinds AND; "Toate" clears), "Înscrierile tale", "Deschise pentru înscriere {n}", "În curând"; empty filter result shows "Niciun eveniment nu se potrivește." with "Arată toate"; pull to refresh; card tap → `eventDetails`, share → `eventShareText`.
- **News**: `StiriDashboard` (search by title/description, filters type/category/period via `FilterStiriScreen`, admin and player), `NewsScreen` (image carousel with fullscreen viewer `FullScreenImageViewer`, category, date, description, optional CTA button from `buttonText`/`buttonLink` via `LinkUtil`, admin edit button → `createNews` with the `stire`), `NewsCategoryManipulationScreen` (search, radio-style selection, add new category, returns the `NewsCategory`).

## Admin
- **AdminHome** — AppBar "Admin Home" + logout; quick action cards (create event, create news, send notification to everyone); "Eveniment live" card per `liveEvents` with "Gestionează eveniment" → `eventManagement`; upcoming events with stats; news carousel (admin variant).
- **CreateEventPage** — fields: image (landing), Nume, Locație, Punct de start, Descriere, Data și ora evenimentului, "Când se deschid înscrierile?", "Când se inchid înscrierile?", Tipul de eveniment (Public/Privat), Cod start, background image for missions, Lungime traseu, Durata, Dificultate. Every field validated non-empty (several via `v!.isEmpty`, will throw on null). Button "Creează eveniment" / "Actualizează eveniment" → `AddMissionsPage`. Date fields format `yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm`.
- **AddMissionsPage** — mission tabs/list via `MissionWidget`; `+` adds a mission (`Mission(id: index)`); "Creează eveniment" (disabled until every mission is complete) saves and returns to `adminHome`. Mission/puzzle widgets get `mode` (`create`/`blueprint`/`edit`).
- **PuzzleWidget** — preview of the puzzle's components + add component (`AddComponentWidget` → text/image/button/code editors, each with live preview and `ColorPickerDialog`), `SavePuzzle` panel (type dropdown; code → answer; geolocation → latitude/longitude; hint "indiciu"). Save shows 'Puzzle salvat cu succes'.
- **EventsDashboard (admin)** — search toggle, filter button with active-filter chips, "Creează eveniment" card, sections "Evenimente viitoare" / "Evenimente trecute", empty text 'Nu există evenimente' / 'Nu s-au găsit evenimente', error + retry ('Încarcă evenimente'). Card tap → `eventDetails` (isAdmin true).
- **EventManagementScreen** — banner states ("Evenimentul nu a început încă. Harta și chat-ul vor fi disponibile după ce evenimentul începe." / "Evenimentul a fost oprit. Echipele au fost anunțate."), buttons: Trimite notificare, map, chat (both disabled before start), "Oprește evenimentul" (confirm dialog "Oprește evenimentul": "Toate echipele vor vedea un mesaj că evenimentul s-a terminat. Ești sigur?").
- **EventRegisteredTeams** — list of `TeamCardWidget` (name, captain, phone, members, delete button) → confirm "Ești sigur că vrei să ștergi echipa înregistrată?"; empty 'Nicio echipă înscrisă'. Delete also removes the event from each member's `registeredEventIds`.
- **TeamChatsScreen** — teams of the event (name, "Captain: …", "Members: …"), empty "No teams available" → `chatScreen`.
- **MapScreen** ("Harta evenimentului") — OSM tiles (`userAgentPackageName` is still the placeholder `com.example.flutter_map_test`), initial centre (45.7833, 24.15) zoom 15 (Sibiu area), one circular marker per team with the first letter of its name; tap → bottom sheet with details, distance from the admin's own position (`_currentPosition`), "Call" (`tel:`) and "Message" (→ chat). Teams that never started sit at (0, 0) and are not filtered out.
- **NotificationScreen** — "Trimite notificare[ — event]", info text when scoped to an event ("Această notificare va fi trimisă doar participanților înscriși la acest eveniment."), Titlu/Descriere → `NetworkAPI.sendNotification`; success/error SnackBar.
- **CreateStireScreen** — title, description, publication date, type (Publică/Privată), category (picker → category screen), optional button text + link, main image, up to several secondary images (existing URLs + new files tracked separately in `CreateStireController`). Validation SnackBars for missing main image / type / category. Save → `NewsList.createStire/updateStire`; messages 'Știrea a fost publicată cu succes!' / 'Știrea a fost actualizată cu succes!'.
- **MenuWidget actions** (event dropdown in `MyAppBar`) — "Editează evenimentul" (`isBlueprint:false` → mode edit), "Duplică eveniment" (blueprint), delete (dialog 'Confirmare ștergere' → `EventList.deleteEvent`, does **not** delete teams/messages/Storage files), "Vezi Eveniment" (game preview).

## Visibility rules actually implemented
- `EventType`/`StireType` (PUBLIC/PRIVAT, PUBLICA/PRIVATA) only change badge colour/label (`EventImage`, `StireCard`) and filter results. **There is no access control** — private events/news are listed to everyone.
- Role is checked only at login redirect; screens/routes themselves do not verify `Role.ADMIN`.
